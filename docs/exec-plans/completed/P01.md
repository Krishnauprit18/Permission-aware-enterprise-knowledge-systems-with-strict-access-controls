## Objective

Define the complete synthetic product contract, trust boundaries, threat model, planned standards verification, and settled security decisions without implementing product features or selecting technologies.

## Delivered

- Normative Northstar Cloud Systems product specification with five departments, three customer accounts, five heterogeneous content types, five classification levels, separate view/share policy, functional and non-functional requirements, update/deletion bounds, and explicit non-goals.
- Ten exact user journeys covering lookup, synthesis, conflict, staleness, refusal, customer-safe output, revocation, hourly updates, deletion/de-permissioning, and indirect prompt injection.
- Normative invariant registry including `INV-AUTHZ-001`, `INV-TENANT-001`, `INV-CITE-001`, `INV-REFUSAL-001`, `INV-ACL-CHANGE-001`, `INV-DELETE-001`, and `INV-PROMPT-001`.
- Trust model covering assets, actors, trust boundaries, entry points, data flows, and trusted/untrusted decisions.
- STRIDE plus LLM/RAG threat register with 21 threats, required mitigations, and planned tests.
- Standards verification matrix for NIST SSDF 1.1, OWASP ASVS 5.0.0, OWASP Top 10:2025, OWASP GenAI/LLM Top 10 2026, and SLSA 1.2 concepts, with no certification claims.
- Four accepted ADRs for authorization-before-context, separate view/share policy, untrusted retrieved content with validated citations, and fail-closed non-disclosing refusal behavior.
