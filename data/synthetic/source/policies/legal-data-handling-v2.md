# Restricted data handling policy v2

Effective 2026-09-01. Approved by Synthetic Northstar Legal.

Restricted customer, contract, and security material may be viewed only by
explicit Legal Counsel grants or an equally explicit approved relationship.
Internal viewing does not authorize external sharing. Customer-shareable output
requires a separate `can_share_externally` decision and the approved audience.

This policy supersedes restricted data handling policy v1.
